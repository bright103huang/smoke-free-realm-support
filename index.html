Smoke-Free Realm Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Data stored on your device

Smoke-Free Realm stores workday start and end times, craving sessions, selected shift cards, smoking records, reward ledger entries, language preference, and privacy acknowledgement in chrome.storage.local on the device where the extension is installed.

The extension does not read page content, tab URLs, tab titles, browsing history, or keystrokes. It does not create an account and does not send product data to a server.

Use and sharing

Data is used only to provide workday tracking, craving support, local reward calculations, settings, and user-requested JSON export. Smoke-Free Realm does not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Your controls

You can export a JSON copy from Settings. Nothing is exported until you select that action. You can permanently delete all extension data from Settings; deletion also resets language and privacy preferences.

Limited Use

Smoke-Free Realm's use of information is limited to providing and improving its single purpose of workday smoking-cessation support. Data is not sold, is not used or transferred for advertising, and is not used or transferred to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Medical boundary and contact

Smoke-Free Realm is a behavior support tool, not a medical device or a guarantee of smoking cessation. Questions can be sent through the public support repository . Do not post passwords, private health information, export files, or other sensitive personal data in an issue.

无烟境界隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

保存在本设备上的数据

无烟境界会在安装扩展的设备上，通过 chrome.storage.local 保存工作日开始和结束时间、烟瘾事件、选用的换挡卡、吸烟记录、奖励流水、语言偏好和隐私确认状态。

本扩展不读取网页内容、标签页 URL、标签页标题、浏览历史或键盘输入；不创建账号，也不向服务器发送产品数据。

使用与共享

上述数据只用于提供工作日记录、烟瘾支持、本地奖励计算、设置和用户主动触发的 JSON 导出。无烟境界不出售数据，不将数据用于广告，也不与第三方共享。

你的控制

你可以在设置中导出 JSON 副本；未主动选择导出时不会生成文件。你也可以在设置中永久删除全部扩展数据；删除后，语言和隐私确认也会恢复默认。

Limited Use 声明

无烟境界对信息的使用仅限于提供和改进“工作日戒烟行为支持”这一单一用途。数据不会被出售，不会被用于或转移用于广告，也不会被用于或转移用于判断信用状况或借贷评估。

医疗边界与联系

无烟境界是行为辅助工具，不是医疗设备，也不保证戒烟成功。如有问题，请通过 公开支持仓库 联系我们。请勿在 issue 中发布密码、私人健康信息、导出文件或其他敏感个人数据。
